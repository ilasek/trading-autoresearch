## The decision rule for "does this object become a recorded scout trial?"

It deliberately contains NO THRESHOLD ON A TRAIN SIGNAL STATISTIC. Grounds, all from
the lab's own record:
  - learnings.md 2026-10-16/17: "a train screen may REFUTE but may not QUALIFY",
    and refutations hold only because "no validation luck overturns ARITHMETIC".
  - learnings.md 2026-11-16, n=27: corr(train skill, validation skill) = -0.08
    pearson / +0.02 spearman. The train number carries no information about the
    outcome it would be used to predict.
  - learnings.md 2026-11-19: the lab's own free region-matched forward-excess
    screen rejects a true null 57% of the time in its most-quoted cell.
The last twelve sessions each declined their object on a sigma-against-a-bar read off
that instrument. That is the qualification use the 2026-10-16/17 rule forbids, applied
in the negative direction, and it has produced zero recorded results since 2026-11-08.

An object is DECLINED only on an ARITHMETIC ground:
  D1 NOT COMPUTABLE / CONTAMINATED. Depends on a stored price or volume LEVEL, or on a
     quantity in which the as-of-fetch back-adjustment factor does not cancel.
  D2 RELABELLING. Within-date cross-sectional Spearman |rho| >= 0.90 to a score already
     measured on this board. (Then it is a refinement of a measured object.)
  D3 COST EXCEEDS THE PRIZE BY CONSTRUCTION. Pre-registered one-way churn implies an
     annual cost bill >= the object's own gross prize, priced at this lab's measured
     ~11 bps/yr per 1x of one-way churn on a K=100 book.
  D4 WRONG PANEL. The source localises the effect to a cell this universe excludes by
     construction (micro caps, low institutional ownership, ...).
  D5 DIRECTION NOT PRE-SIGNABLE from the mechanism alone.
Otherwise the object is RUN. A weak-looking train screen is reported with the trial and
is NOT a reason to decline.

## AMENDMENT, after the free score-level screen and BEFORE any trial ran

Three changes. Each is forced by an arithmetic or already-measured ground, not by a
result I preferred; the screen scored no returns, so nothing below is fitted to an outcome.

A1. O2 `rv_volofvol_raw_control` is DECLINED on D2, my own rule, measured at +0.964.
    The log construction of vol-of-vol is already almost vol-level-free (rho to vol126
    = +0.132), so removing the explicit tercile demeaning changes the score by almost
    nothing and the "paired control" would have been an algebraically-near-identical
    relative of O1 — the thing the 2026-10-26/27 method rule forbids shipping.

A2. O3's winsorised-cumsum volume index is DECLINED on D1 — MY OWN FIX IS THE
    CONTAMINANT. The clip at +/- log(1.5) binds on 7.16% of eligible printing days
    (median |daily log volume change| is 0.254, so the clip sits at only 1.6x the median
    daily move), and because the index is a CUMSUM every clipped day shifts its level
    permanently. The result is an accumulated random walk of clipping residuals, not a
    volume shock: rank correlation to the plain log-volume shock is +0.126.
    REPLACED BY THE LITERATURE'S OWN WINDOW, which is also the D1-clean one: shock =
    mean log volume over the last 5 days minus the MEDIAN over days -6..-50 (Gervais-
    Kaniel-Mingelgrin's 1-day-against-49-day shape). A ~50-day total window makes the
    chance a split step falls inside it ~50/252 x a ~3%/yr split rate ~ 0.6% of
    name-dates, and an explicit causal split guard drops any name with a daily
    |log volume change| > log(3) anywhere in the window (0.57% of days).
    Recorded as a structural fact about this panel, not as a defeat: THE CLEAN CORNER OF
    THE VOLUME PANEL AND THE CHEAP CORNER OF THE COST MODEL DO NOT OVERLAP. Long-window
    within-name volume ratios carry the split step; short-window ones are clean and
    churn. Revised turnover range: 2.0 - 6.0x/yr.

A3. O1 DROPS THE SIX OVERLAPPING SLEEVES, on the record's own measurement rather than
    on a preference. learnings F5 of 2026-10-27/28 applied that operator to three slow
    RISK scores and measured it collapsing the gross term (lowvol126 +0.057 -> +0.010
    gross), because the operator needs a score whose content is older than the holding
    period and a 252-day statistic read five months late is a window shifted by half its
    own length. Vol-of-vol is a 252-day statistic, so the operator is refuted for it in
    advance. REPLACED BY the monthly 200-name RETENTION BAND, which re-times nothing and
    which the record tells any new signal to carry. Turnover range unchanged at 0.6-1.8x.
    The tercile demeaning is kept exactly as pre-registered and is a provable no-op
    within the quota's own cells (it subtracts a per-tercile constant, and the quota
    cells are peer x tercile), so the shipped book is the pre-registered one.

A4. A THIRD OBJECT, replacing the declined O2, pre-registered here before it is written:
    O4 `pt_lowvol126_quota_band` family price-trend. O1's harness byte-identical with the
    score replaced by the trailing 126-day volatility LEVEL (low held). It is the paired
    control that A1 showed vol-of-vol actually needs: the pair differs in one score on
    identical dates in an identical harness, so it isolates whether vol-of-vol carries
