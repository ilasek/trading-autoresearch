## Top 3 learnings

1. **`portfolio-learning` (combining multiple signals into one "union" book) is closed as inert.** Nine trials built a 0–4 leg ladder of combined signals; validation Sharpe rose monotonically with leg *count* (0.759 → 0.958), but three independent controls showed the mechanism doesn't care what the legs actually are: a book built from **random hashes instead of real signals** scored within noise of the real 4-leg book (t=1.11), swapping in a leg measured +0.236 better standalone moved the combined book by essentially zero (t=-0.03), and train/validation disagreed about the shape entirely (train peaks at 2 legs, validation is monotone to 4). Recommendation on record: stop building union books.
2. **Concentration cuts the wrong way outside the momentum family.** In `price-trend`, more concentration (fewer, higher-conviction names) has reliably helped. In the new `liquidity-volume` lead, the opposite held across a 3x breadth bracket: widening from 25 to 37 names and cutting HHI 31% *raised* validation Sharpe (0.917 → 0.942), while narrowing to 12 names cost 0.043. `CLAUDE.md`'s warning not to carry `price-trend`'s constants to other families by analogy is now demonstrated with the sign flipped, not just "untested."
3. **`range-variance` is a family the program can't actually explore on this data, and the lab has now said so for five sessions running.** Twelve distinct range/spread-based mechanisms (Parkinson vol, Corwin-Schultz spread, FHT, several liquidity commonality ideas) have all been screened and killed on identified causes — mostly survivorship-inflated volatility levels or insufficient names-per-region for the statistic to work at all. `program.md`'s "at least 1 trial in any zero-trial family" rule can't be satisfied for this one without spending a trial the lab is confident is wasted; this needs a human decision to amend the rule or formally retire the family.
