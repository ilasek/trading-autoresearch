## Top 3 learnings

1. **A four-time-running validation/holdout split reversal traces to one commit, not a drift.** Every promotion before trial #43 moved validation and holdout together; every one since has moved them in opposite directions. The dividing line is exactly when six overlapping monthly formation tranches were replaced by a single tranche — a change that concentrates the book and, per the year-by-year decomposition, pulls over half its validation gain from the single best year (2020) in the six-year validation window.
2. **Weight concentration and risk concentration are different things, and this repo's diagnostics had been measuring the wrong one.** A holdings-only risk-contribution statistic (no returns scored) showed the champion's top position carries a much larger share of portfolio *variance* than of *capital* — and correctly predicted, before the trial ran, that removing this week's membership buffer would widen drawdown even though a simpler "fringe weight" diagnostic had said to expect nothing.
3. **Two headline mechanisms in six days turned out not to exist as described, both caught for free by reading the engine code rather than trusting the narrative.** The champion's "vol-spike trim reads its own basket" claim was wrong (it was reading a near-fixed set of old-economy stocks via a data-availability accident); this week a second claim — that the engine lets portfolio weights "drift" with prices between monthly rebalances — was also retracted after a diagnostic showed the engine forward-fills to a genuinely constant-weight book. Both cost several trials before being caught.

## What changes in this report's reading

- **The week's headline concern does not reproduce under v2.** The report's alarm was that four consecutive promotions raised validation while holdout fell (1.377 → 0.691). On the point-in-time universe neither half holds: the three promotions after #42 *lower* validation on both v2 views (masked 0.63 → 0.54 → 0.54 → 0.53, ported 0.47 → 0.41 → 0.36 → 0.36), and ported holdout does not deteriorate (1.35 for #42, 1.38–1.42 for #43–#51). The validation gains and the holdout drop were both v1 artefacts.
- **Under v2 none of the week's promotions improves on `mom_12m_baseline` on the ported comparison**, and none of the seven books clears the 90% null (ported percentiles 29–82%). Across all eight champions, validation→holdout rank agreement moves from −0.33 (v1) to +0.54 (v2 ported), so the splits disagree less once survivorship is removed.
