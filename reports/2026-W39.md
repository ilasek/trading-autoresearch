## Top 3 learnings

1. **A decorrelation lever that is neither de-concentration nor breadth.** Ranking on `E/Var` (hedgeable fraction of variance) selects names the rest of the universe can't replicate, cutting book volatility by lowering co-movement — not by changing concentration, exposure, or name count. This is what carried the promotion, and every other de-risking construction the lab has tried worked through exposure or membership count, not this axis.
2. **A parameter-count triage rule transfers out of its home family, and its cost has a mechanism.** "Estimating nothing beats estimating something" (previously only measured inside `price-trend`) was tested in `range-variance`: swapping `1/σ²` sizing for equal weight on an identical name-set bought **+0.421 of validation Sharpe**. The estimation error doesn't arrive as scatter — it arrives as concentration (HHI up 7.2x), the same failure mode as an unshrunk inverse-covariance weight.
3. **"Quality"/orthogonality gates built on a global market regression are a beta bet in disguise; the same idea built locally isn't.** Twice this week (once on `E/Var`'s locality curve, once independently on an "estimate-quality" operator), a globally-estimated statistic reduced algebraically to β̂'s relative (`R²=ρ²`, `β=ρσ/σ_m`) while the locally-estimated version passed the same gate cleanly. Any future screen built on a regression statistic should be checked against β̂ before being trusted.

## Notable failures worth remembering

- **The direct market-correlation score (`mc`) failed a beta-orthogonality gate its own designers added that morning** (spearman 0.675 vs a 0.50 bar) — then a locality sweep showed `mc` and the seated `E/Var` are the *same object at different neighbourhood sizes*, closing the "is there a better direct object" question for zero trials.
- **Peer-momentum lead-lag scores (0.339, 0.359) burned over half their gross return on turnover** (18.6x annualized) — but not because of neighborhood re-estimation (freezing the peer set changed churn by 0.0005); the 21-day signal horizon was the entire cost, the same cost wall the lab has hit on reversal constructions before.
- **A volatility-targeting overlay on a diversified 42-ETF sleeve was nearly inert** — the constraint almost never bound, because a diversified basket's own realized vol rarely exceeds the target; a de-risking overlay can only be tested where the constraint binds.
- **`DR` (diversification ratio) was retired as a drawdown predictor** on its first pre-registered test (wrong-signed slope, R²=0.15, n=4) — kept only as a composition descriptor.
- **A branch/merge housekeeping gap has quietly cost 4 of the last 18 sessions.** The session-start hook and the harness disagree with `CLAUDE.md`/`program.md` about which branch to use; a post-session forensic audit found 4 of 14 stranded per-run branches required a manual recovery merge by the *next* session, not an automatic pipeline as previously assumed — still unresolved.

# Re-scoring note — 2026-10-05 (protocol v2)

Everything above was measured under protocol v1, on today's ~140 constituents — survivors by
construction. The protocol-v2 re-scoring (merged 2026-10-05, ilasek/trading-autoresearch#4) re-ran
all 104 recorded trials on a point-in-time universe, where a strategy sees a name only while it was
