## Top 3 learnings

1. **"Blending beats switching" now covers timing on a component's *own* past return, not just external state variables.** A designed pair of twelve-sleeve books — identical except one drops a sleeve when its own trailing-12-month return is negative — showed the timed version winning cleanly on train (+1.55%/yr, t=+2.25, sign survives all six leave-one-out drops, matched hash placebo at 0%) but **losing** on validation (−0.112 Sharpe, t≈−1.6). This is the fourth consecutive instance of "clean train screen, no validation transfer" the lab has recorded, and the cleanest one yet. The always-on control (0.867 validation) is now the second-best non-`price-trend` result on the board.
2. **The DSR deflator's effective-trial count may not match this repo's actual search structure — flagged for a human, not treated as a bug.** Eigenvalue/participation-ratio analysis of the 90 stored validation return series puts the effective number of independent bets at **1.6 (raw) to 4.4 (with the long-only PC1 constraint removed)**, against the engine's clustering-based count of 24. The clustering threshold (`TRIAL_CLUSTER_RHO = 0.95`, in frozen `engine/protocol.py`) sits at the 91st percentile of the repo's own pairwise correlation distribution — the code matches its documented "deliberately conservative" intent, so this is explicitly not an engine issue, but the lab flags that a low effective-N reading also cuts the other way (the deflator likely *under*-corrects against the wider literature universe the hypotheses are drawn from) and recommends no bar change pending a human decision.
3. **A mis-specified `garman_klass_vol` call was found inside a committed `strategies/lib/` file and deliberately left unfixed.** `sleeve_book.py:41` passes arguments in the wrong order, silently binding `close = 21` (the window, as an integer) instead of the price panel; it still runs without error and only rank-correlates +0.06 with the correct calculation. It feeds two of the twelve sleeves behind last week's second-best non-`price-trend` result (0.867). The trial's own conclusion is unaffected (the error cancels in the paired timed-vs-untimed contrast), but `CLAUDE.md`'s rule against editing existing lib files binds exactly here — fixing it in place would silently change two already-recorded trials. A human may want to retire the file by name.

## Notable failures worth remembering

- **`lead-lag-spillover` is now closed in both halves.** The regional half's Lo–MacKinlay non-trading model was rejected on every partition of the universe this week — including two partitions where no timezone offset could even exist — worse than its own no-lag-rejection null. The surviving cross-region violations turn out to sit exactly behind the engine's 1-day execution lag, so they're the trading calendar, not an exploitable pattern.
- **`beta-minus` and coskewness both close on evidence, with no trial spent.** `beta-minus` correlates +0.60 with the plain volatility level already ruled a survivorship artifact and collapses to noise inside a volatility tercile — the tenth time that control has killed a candidate in this family. Coskewness came in wrong-signed in every subperiod and failed its own pre-registered directional falsifier.
- **`pl_factor_momentum_timed/untimed`** — see Top Learning #1; recorded as `SCOUT`/`SCOUT`, not a rejection of the family (`portfolio-learning`'s lead stays at 1.008).
- **A signed sort where both ends beat the pool turned out to be the survivorship-dispersion artifact again**, the ninth time that shape has shown up under a different name — this time in an overnight-sentiment reversal score whose "long" and "short" legs were both positive.
