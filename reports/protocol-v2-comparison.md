# Correction — 2026-10-06: 27 trials were confined to survivors

## What was wrong

`strategies/lib/groups.py` hard-codes a sector map of the 140 legacy instruments (98 stocks),
and builds its region and type maps from `data/universe.yaml` — today's constituents. A score
that keys on those maps can only rank the names in them:

- `signal_blend.group_lead_score` scores only names in the sector map;
- the union, intersection and integrated books (`signal_blend`, `union_legs`) keep only names
  **every** leg can score, so their pool *is* the legacy list;
- the region- and type-demeaned scores (`lv_illiq_region_*`, the E/Var books, the v1 champion)
  drop any name whose region or type the map does not know.

The masked view (V2m) hid non-members' prices from these books, but it could not widen a pool
the code had already narrowed to survivors. Their random-selection null, meanwhile, drew from
the whole point-in-time pool. So for these trials the null percentile compares a survivors-only
book with random books that are not survivors-only, and the v2 Sharpe is a survivorship-biased
number on a point-in-time calendar.

**Measured, holdings only** (validation window, prices to 2023-12-31, the re-scoring's masked
view; no returns scored, nothing recorded; `experiments/protocol_v2/legacy_share.py`, results in
`legacy_share.jsonl` beside it): the 98 legacy stocks are **9.4%** of the
eligible stocks on an average validation day. 27 trials put **100%** of
their stock weight on them; across the other trials that hold stocks the median is
**15%** (plain 12-1 momentum: 15%). There is no
middle ground: every other book that holds stocks sits between 0% and 24%.

30 trials import `groups.py`; 26 of them are confined. The other
4 use it in a way that does not restrict stock selection — `pl_2leg_content_partner` (#73), `pl_2leg_null_partner` (#74), `rv_sleeve_voltarget_phi05` (#95), `pl_2leg_placebo_partner` (#75) —
and their published v2 numbers stand. `mom_multihorizon_zscore_sectorneutral` (#23) is confined by a sector map of its own.

The cleanest demonstration is in the report itself: `pl_zeroleg_all_placebo` (#77) replaces every
signal with a hash of date and ticker — it reads no market data — and lands at validation
0.77, the 100th null percentile, rank
6 of 104. Random picks from the survivor pool beat random picks from the full
pool, and every confined trial inherited that tailwind.

Criterion 1's recommended guard ("refuse instrument literals in candidate source") would not have
caught this: the literals were in a library module. The guard installed at the v2 cut-over
(`engine/protocol.py`, `static_check`) follows `strategies` imports transitively and refuses every
trial above that imports `groups.py`, confined or not, as well as any own sector map.

## What changes in this report's reading

- **The "biggest risers" table is an artefact.** 9 of the ten risers are confined —
  the lead-lag, seasonality and portfolio-learning books rose because their pool stayed survivors
  while everyone else's widened. None of them is evidence that those families survive
  point-in-time selection. `pt_raw_reversal_control` (#65) is unconfined; it fell (0.70 → 0.57) and rose in rank only because the field fell further.
- **The "biggest fallers" table mixes both kinds.** 7 of the ten are confined (the
  E/Var books, three illiquidity books, `pt_mom_seasonal_deferral`, `pt_mom_id_z`); their
  v2 numbers are not clean measurements. `mom_hzn_avg4_phase4`, `mom_zscore_hzn_geom4_k1`, `mom_hzn_avg4_weekly_resize` are unconfined and did fall.
- **The order changed far less than published.** Among unconfined trials v1 and v2 rank agreement
  is +0.74, not +0.43. v2 roughly halves almost every unconfined trial's Sharpe
  (median +0.90 → +0.48) but largely preserves the order — the momentum lineage
  stays on top.
