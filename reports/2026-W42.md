## Top 3 learnings

1. **A train-side qualification bar was proposed and refuted inside two sessions.** Wednesday
   night calibrated "train selection term ≥ +0.24" as the threshold to expect clearing the
   validation null gate, from one paired comparison. Thursday night tested it against all
   seven v3 trials: correlation between train skill and validation selection is **−0.345**
   (Spearman −0.571) — the wrong sign, and not reliably different from zero at n=7. Verdict:
   train screens can **refute** (cost arithmetic, relabelling correlation, sign-flip tests)
   but cannot **qualify or rank** a candidate pre-validation. Stop building screens meant to
   bless one.
2. **Birru's weekday-mood effect replicates here at the characteristic level (7/7 legs
   pre-signed, 5 at |t|>3) but is pure noise at the name level**, and even the characteristic
   version is unharvestable: its alpha is 85–100% concentrated on Monday, and rotating to
   capture it costs 30.67%/yr on 131.7x turnover against a ~300bps/yr gross prize. Closes
   `seasonality-calendar`'s day-of-week axis three independent ways in one night.
3. **A long-formation-window score's "geography" (null-median-minus-pool) term can be mostly
   a warm-up artifact**, not a region effect — the null's replicas inherit the candidate's own
   un-invested ramp-up while the equal-weight pool is invested from day one. Both recorded
   geography terms to date need re-reading against each candidate's first emitted row.
