## Top 3 learnings

1. **Active Share and `rho` are a pair, and the first calibration point says the seat's top-ten channel sets both.** Two one-node changes to the champion one night apart bracket it: a change moving 8.27% of gross at rank 15–25 landed at `rho` 0.9984 / `t` 0.49; a change displacing three of the top ten holdings (Active Share 0.2566) landed at `rho` 0.9869 / `t` 1.95 — four times the weight-space distance, an order of magnitude more `t`. Use Active Share only as a description/falsifier (near zero ⇒ cannot resolve), never as evidence of expected decorrelation gain.
2. **A screen that cleanly passes a candidate's mechanism is still never evidence the candidate will win.** `ID_Z` cleared this lab's best-performing orthogonality screen by 3x the required margin, in the exact shape the source literature predicts — and then cost −0.127 net of confounds, losing in 5 of 6 validation years. This is the seventh instance of "liveness passing ⇒ no predictive content," and the first on a score (not averaging) axis. Standing rule: **use every screen to kill, never to forecast.**
3. **The turnover/cost-mitigation axis is now closed across the whole board, not just the champion.** Every no-trade band, deferral rule, and reduced-frequency overlay tested this cycle fails because the seat's book replaces the majority of itself monthly (Jaccard ≤0.27) — a remedy that re-times trades can't reach a book that turns over that fast regardless. This reopens only if the champion's own turnover or volatility materially changes.
