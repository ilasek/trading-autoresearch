## Top 3 learnings

1. **Validation and holdout have opposite resolving power in this family, and the lab proved it before the human acted on it.** Free analysis on 08-25 error-barred both splits for the first time: on validation, not one of the champion's six predecessors is statistically distinguishable from it (largest |t| = 1.98); on holdout, five of six beat it at |t| > 2.20–3.22. That result — not just "the numbers look worse" — is what motivated the holdout veto added the next day.
2. **The four-horizon momentum family is now fully mapped on its own base.** Two trials this week closed the last two open construction questions: whether the magnitude-weighting transform's edge is ordering or spacing (55–62% ordering, reversing an earlier read from a smaller base), and whether the four lookback legs need to nest (disjoint legs cost -0.037, "half-price" between two previously calibrated constants). Every remaining component — signal, kernel shape, leg count/nesting/weighting, membership band, weight anchor, tranche depth, rebalance cadence, trim overlay — now has a measured marginal value, and all sit at a local optimum or a refuted alternative.
3. **The risk-contribution diagnostic (effective risk bets vs. drawdown) has a structural blind spot, found by re-fitting it on the new base.** It's calibrated correctly on the base it was fit on but 35–55% too steep elsewhere, because it's computed on a single-date normalized weight vector and so cannot see exposure-scalar overlays or formation-date diversification — both of which move drawdown. Practical effect: keep using the diagnostic's sign, stop trusting its slope across bases.

## Notable failures worth remembering

- **A validation-only gate was gamed by its own family for three straight promotions.** The three candidates rolled back this week each won the only contest the old gate held (rising validation Sharpe) while holdout fell every time, ending at Sharpe 0.691 — the worst in the repo's history. The fix (a veto, not a new objective) is deliberately narrow: holdout is still never ranked or maximized, only used to block a clear loser, to avoid burning the one split that hasn't been spent yet.
- Two research ideas from `research/SUMMARY.md` were screened this week without spending a trial: regional-neutral momentum ranking (real premise, but its best case is below the family's resolution floor and unreachable without fitting to the scoring split — closed) and a negative-5-year-return "value" proxy (the most decorrelated construction ever measured here, but concentrated in a ~10-name European-financials/energy cast that the lab flags as the single most survivorship-flattered object it could build — screened, not decided).
